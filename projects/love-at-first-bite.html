The game

Love at First Bite is a dating sim with a focus on narrative and investigation. You live the life of a vampire surviving through a dating app: swipe right on tasty humans, swipe left when a profile feels off — some vampires are better at hiding than others. Matches are limited each day, and your thirst only grows while you decide on tonight's dinner.

From jam to production

The game started as our entry to the Indie Spain Jam 2023. The concept held up, so we took it through the PowerUp+ pre-incubation program (Oct–Dec 2023) and founded Honey Bun Games to build the full version, in production since 2024.

Design highlights

  • Deduction as the core verb — profiles are puzzles. Reading bios, photos and chat behaviour to unmask hidden vampires turns a swipe into a risk/reward decision.
  • Pressure economy — limited daily matches and a growing thirst meter create a soft timer that forces commitment without hard fail states.
  • Dates as minigames — conversations run on a click-and-drag tension bar: keep the date charmed, don't let suspicion peak.

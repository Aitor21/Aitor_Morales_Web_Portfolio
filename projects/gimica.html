01

THE STUDIO

Gimica is a Berlin games studio founded in 2021 that creates and publishes casual games enjoyed by a very wide audience. Its games power JustPlay, a loyalty app where players earn real-world rewards for playtime — a portfolio of 35+ games with over 25 million downloads and 500,000+ daily active users. I'm the portfolio's sole game designer, working across game design, development, product, UX and QA — often on the same feature.

02

SYSTEMS THAT SCALE

The most leveraged work is reusable systems design. I've designed and shipped boosters, combo systems, rewarded-video minigames and chest-reward systems that are deployed across many titles — designed once, themed per game, tuned per audience.

03

LEVELS BY THE HUNDREDS

I designed the adaptive difficulty and level-progression systems and have authored hundreds of levels. At portfolio scale, level design becomes a pipeline problem as much as a craft problem: difficulty curves that keep players challenged but never stuck, and authoring approaches that keep variety high and production fast.

04

HYPOTHESIS TO ROLLOUT

  • Plan and run A/B tests end to end — hypothesis, variant design, rollout, readout.
  • Own features from design through implementation, QA and launch.
  • Prioritize roadmaps and coordinate across art, development and QA.
  • Started a recurring cross-functional design review for the team.

05

TECHNICAL DESIGN

I build what I design: shipped features in Unity, with additional work in Babylon.js and Godot, and engineered tools and game systems solo from prototype to release. Specs are written for hand-off — clear enough that engineering and art can build from them without me in the room.

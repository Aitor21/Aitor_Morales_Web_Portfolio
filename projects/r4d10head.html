01

THE GAME

R4D10HEAD is a stealth game with prop-hunt-style interactions. Our little robot can't help being a nuisance: movement is all-or-nothing — full speed, full volume — and standing still means going blind while it recharges. Avoid the CORPORATION's guards and cameras and find the way out.

02

INVERTED STEALTH

Classic stealth gives you tools to be quiet; R4D10HEAD takes them away. The core loop trades information for mobility: while moving you're deafeningly loud but can see; while stopped you're safe but blind. That single inversion drives every design decision — guards react to noise you cannot avoid making, so the skill is when to be loud, not how to stay silent.

03

LEVEL DESIGN

  • Guard and camera placement built around readable patrol rhythms players can learn while blind.
  • Prop-interaction spots that reward planning a route before committing to noise.
  • Escalating spaces: teaching rooms first, then layered patrols where routes must be chained.

R4D10HEAD

Stealth, except you are the loudest thing in the room. Move and the whole floor hears you; stand still and you go blind. Every route is a bet.

What I built

I held game design and level design on the project. The level work came down to three decisions, all of them downstream of one rule: the player cannot move and see at the same time.

  • Guard and camera placement built around readable patrol rhythms a player can learn while blind.
  • Prop-interaction spots that reward planning a route before committing to the noise.
  • Escalating spaces: teaching rooms first, then layered patrols where routes have to be chained.
02 — The problem

Take the quiet away

The inversion

Classic stealth hands you tools to be quiet. R4D10HEAD takes them away. Our little robot cannot help being a nuisance: movement is all or nothing, full speed at full volume, and standing still means going blind while it recharges.

The core loop therefore trades information for mobility. While you move you are deafeningly loud but you can see; while you stop you are safe but blind. Guards react to noise you cannot avoid making, so the skill on offer is when to be loud, not how to stay silent.

What that forces on the level

A player who is blind while safe cannot read a room in the moment — they have to have read it already. Every space has to be legible in the short, loud windows when the lights are on, and survivable in the long, dark ones when they are not.

Slip past the CORPORATION's guards and cameras, and find the way out.

03 — Substructure

The blockout beneath

The rule above dictates the plan. Rooms are chained so a route can be rehearsed before it is committed to, and the teaching space sits between the spawn and everything that can kill you.

Illustrative schematic of how the level is structured — spawn, teaching room, prop cover, layered patrols, egress — not a capture of the shipped level. The dotted line is the critical path; nodes mark the spawn, the exit, and the points where a player has to stop, go blind, and re-plan.

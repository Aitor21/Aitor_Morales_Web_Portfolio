01

THE PROJECT

Bliss Pictures, a film production company, set out to bring one of its stories to games. I joined to help make the demo of Luzia: Rainbow in the Darkness real — first as a consultant advising the studio's leadership on how game development actually works, then as a game developer on the demo itself.

02

GAME DESIGN

  • Conceptualized and designed mechanics, gameplay systems and rules with engagement and challenge in mind.
  • Wrote the game design documents — concept, story integration, mechanics, characters and levels.
  • Prototyped mechanics directly in-engine to bridge communication between design and programming.
  • Balanced gameplay, difficulty and player progression throughout.

03

LEVEL DESIGN

  • Designed and built levels end to end: layout, terrain, lighting and gameplay elements.
  • Balanced difficulty and pacing for a challenging-but-fair experience aligned with the narrative.
  • Kept levels within the technical requirements and limitations of the production.

04

CAMERAS & CINEMATICS

  • Designed and animated camera movements — pans, zooms, tilts — to highlight key moments.
  • Implemented multiple camera modes matched to the game's genre and mechanics.
  • Scripted events and cutscenes that carry the story between gameplay beats.

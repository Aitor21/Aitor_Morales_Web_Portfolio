Birds of a Feather

The world used to be kind to pigeons — war heroes, peace bringers, companions. Now they're pests. Legend says one pigeon can unite the flock and bring the glory back. I made all of it, alone.

The game

Fly across the city as the chosen pigeon, win over scattered birds, and grow a flock big enough to take on the enemy leaders who keep pigeonkind down. Part 3D exploration, part crowd-combat — your followers are your health bar, your army and your responsibility.

Why it matters in this portfolio

Everything in the build is mine: concept, systems, flocking follower AI, enemy leaders, level layout, UI, dialogue and the web build itself — scoped, built and shipped within jam deadlines. It's the clearest proof of range: I can design a game and then actually make it exist.

Design notes

  • Followers as a resource — recruiting birds is progression, combat power and risk all in one number.
  • Flight as a verb — traversal is the reward loop; the city is designed around perch-to-perch hops with landmark navigation.
  • Honest jank — the itch page documents known bugs openly; shipping and transparency beat polish theatre in jam builds.

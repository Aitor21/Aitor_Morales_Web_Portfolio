DEV COMMENTARY · THE DESIGNER BEHIND IT

Commentary

The designer appears after the work. Order is information.

Madrid-based game & level designer. Designer and developer at Gimica (JustPlay), shipping features across a portfolio of live mobile games with art, engineering and product. Co-founder of Honey Bun Games, where we made Love at First Bite. I design systems, then build them.

Software Engineering first, then Game & Interactive Media Design at U-tad. I never fully picked a side between design and code — that refusal became the job description.
Reusable gameplay systems — boosters, combos, chest and reward systems, rewarded-video minigames — built once, integrated into game after game. Plus adaptive difficulty, level progression, and A/B tests run end to end, from hypothesis to rollout.
Founded with two teammates after Indie Spain Jam 2023. Through the PowerUp+ pre-incubation program to a Steam page. Love at First Bite is the proof.
Two years with a film studio, building the playable demo of "Luzia: Rainbow in the Darkness" — game feel in service of someone else's story.
Madrid, Spain. Remote-friendly, jam-hardened, ships in English and Spanish.

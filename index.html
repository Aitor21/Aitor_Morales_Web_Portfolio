02 — Method

Every game starts as a collision

Design DNA

Papers, Please × Reigns = Love at First Bite

Take the suspicious reading of documents from Papers, Please, the swipe-or-die tension of Reigns, wrap it in a dating app — and you get a vampire deciding whether tonight's match is a partner, a friend, or dinner.

How I work

  • Own it end to end. From the spec, through the prototype, into implementation and QA, out to an A/B test that says whether it worked.
  • Design isn't finished when it's written. It's finished when players feel it.
  • Systems thinking is the throughline. The best feature composes cleanly with everything else: reusable, tunable, readable by the team.
03 — About

The designer behind it

Madrid-based game & level designer. I studied Software Engineering and then Game & Interactive Media Design at U-tad, which is why I've never fully picked a side between design and code. That mix became the job.

Today I'm a designer and developer at Gimica, a JustPlay studio, shipping features across a portfolio of live mobile games alongside art, engineering, product and QA. Before that I co-founded Honey Bun Games, where we made Love at First Bite.

WHO I AM

I'm Aitor Morales, a game & level designer based in Madrid. I studied Software Engineering and then Game & Interactive Media Design at U-tad, which is why I've never fully picked a side between design and code — that mix became the job.

Today I'm the sole game designer for a large mobile free-to-play portfolio at Gimica (Berlin), where I design economy systems and boosters, author hundreds of levels, and ship what I spec in Unity, Godot and Babylon.js. Before that I helped film studio Bliss Pictures build the playable demo of Luzia: Rainbow in the Darkness, and with Honey Bun Games — the indie team I co-founded — I'm making Love at first Bite.

I am a proactive person who loves learning new things and experiencing challenges. Teamwork, attention to detail and communication are things that I strive for in every project. My interests lie in photography, game design, UX/UI design, film analysis and cooking.

02

PASSION

  • DESIGN

    I think design is like a "magic wand". If a service or product is magic to solve a problem, it is the role of design to act as an intermediary for users to master it. We strive every day to create a "magic wand" that matches the users needs or desires.

  • TECHNOLOGY

    Technology has the power to change the lives of the world and individuals. As Arthur C. Clarke wrote, "Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic." I want to be a person who can always catch up with the latest technology and respond to changes. I think it is the mission of designers as well as engineers to promote innovation.

  • STORIES

    I love watching TV shows, movies, playing games and reading manga. Stories can get people's attention and make things easier to understand and remember. I believe that any type of designer should consume every type of media to expand their minds and find new angles on how to tell their story.

About Me

Designer, builder,
occasional pigeon.

I'm Aitor Morales, a game & level designer based in Madrid. I design systems and levels for live mobile games by day and build my own vampire dating sim by night — and I've been making games, in teams and alone, for over a decade.

The short story

I studied Software Engineering and then Game & Interactive Media Design at U-tad, which is why I've never fully picked a side between design and code. That mix became the job: today I'm the sole game designer for a large mobile free-to-play portfolio at Gimica (a JustPlay company), where I design economy systems and boosters, author hundreds of levels, and ship what I spec — in Unity, Godot and Babylon.js.

Before that I helped film studio Bliss Pictures build the playable demo of Luzia: Rainbow in the Darkness, doing game design, level design, cameras and cutscenes. And with Honey Bun Games, the indie team I co-founded, I'm making Love at First Bite — a vampire dating sim born in a game jam that refused to stay there.

Teamwork, attention to detail and clear communication are the things I hold myself to on every project. Outside of games: photography, film analysis and cooking — all of which are just design with different constraints.

How I work

Design that ships

A mechanic isn't finished when it's clever — it's finished when it's specced, built, tested and live. I own features end to end, from the first prototype to the A/B readout, and I write documentation that engineering and art can build from without me in the room.

Technology as a design tool

I prototype my own ideas in-engine because the fastest way to evaluate a design is to play it. Knowing how things are built keeps my designs honest — and lets me build the tools and systems myself when that's the shortest path.

Stories everywhere

Games, films, manga, a good meal — stories make things easier to understand and impossible to forget. I steal structure shamelessly from every medium I love, because a level is a story the player tells themselves.
